## Isolated real-browser journeys

A disposable instance started on 0.1.7, then upgraded using the new plugin.
All 23 native participant accounts, five provisional teams, memberships and
25 demo challenges were preserved. A participant registered through the native
form and checked in through the branded app on a 390px device viewport.

Organizers saved capacity, closed check-in and reviewed a 20-attendee plan.
A subsequent personal check-in made that plan stale; applying it failed without
changing membership. A fresh 21-attendee preview produced 5/4/4/4/4 groups.
No-show accounts stayed intact and unassigned. A participant claimed the team's
name through the confirmation dialog; another teammate could not replace it.
Other teams chose names through their own member sessions. A late attendee was
placed with an organizer reason, then rosters were finalized through the UI.
Membership writes were refused; loaded demo content still blocked competition
start independently of roster readiness.

After removing the demo and authoring two native QA challenges in that disposable
instance, event start succeeded. Direct challenge content remained inaccessible
until Start room. The recorded room start and first solve were shared with a
second participant session, unlocking the next step for that teammate.

Both new participant and organizer attendance screens had zero automated WCAG
A/AA violations or incomplete checks. The 390px participant viewport had no
horizontal overflow. Reduced-motion preference produced zero navigation
transition duration. These are development checks, not a substitute for testing
on the venue's actual devices and network.
